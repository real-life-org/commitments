# The core

*People promise, circles hold the space.*

The core without any of the edges: liquid and purpose-bound promises, trust, vouching and circles. Land, app, legal form and the why come later.

Deutsch: [core.de.md](core.de.md)

## The core

People give promises. To a person: I will deliver this to you by then. This promise is liquid: it has a holder and can be passed on until someone redeems it or it expires. Or to a common cause: I will do this for us. This promise is purpose-bound: it has no holder, and only the one who gave it redeems it, by doing it.

Others accept promises because they know who promises and see what this person has kept so far. Whoever vouches for someone accepts their promises. Whoever has redeemed a promise, or has seen that something was done, confirms it. Every promise kept becomes a piece of trust.

Anything larger than two is a circle: a shared intention and the space in which promises towards it are visible. A circle does nothing. It does not promise, confirm or vouch. Whoever visibly contributes to it is part of it and has a share in what emerges. Every person is in many circles, and that is how circles connect: through the people who are in both, or through a new circle they form together.

## Who does what

| | Person | Circle |
|---|---|---|
| gives promises | yes | never |
| accepts promises | yes | never, it only makes them visible |
| redeems | yes | never |
| confirms | yes | never |
| vouches | yes | never |
| holds claims | yes, as a person, and stands for them | never, not even a kitty of promises |
| has rules | – | yes, set by its people: who contributes, how shares arise |

## Two kinds, one promise

A promise is always the same: who promises whom what by when. The two kinds differ only in the "whom". A promise to a person has a holder and is therefore liquid. A promise to a circle has no holder and is therefore purpose-bound. Everything else follows from circles doing nothing.

| | Liquid promise (to a person) | Purpose-bound promise (to a circle) |
|---|---|---|
| Example | "I will deliver you 50 loaves by June." | "I will do X for our project." |
| Who holds it | the recipient, as a claim | nobody, it is visible in the circle |
| Transferable | yes, from hand to hand | no |
| Redeemed | when the holder asks for it | when the giver does it |
| If nothing happens | expires | stays empty, expires |
| Who confirms | whoever redeemed it | whoever saw that it was done |
| What comes back | what was promised | a share in what emerges, without anyone promising it |
