# Commitments instead of money

A community holds land, work and trust in common instead of trading them. Inside, the confirmed contribution counts; outside, the transferable promise. No interest, no speculation, no growth imperative.

This repository is the shared picture for the projects that could build this together, before and during the conversations between them. Nobody has confirmed participation yet. The why, the invitation, is deliberately missing from this draft; it grows out of the conversation.

Deutsch: [README.de.md](README.de.md) · Read it rendered: <https://real-life.org/commitments/>

## The model

People give promises: to a person (liquid, passed on until redeemed or expired) or to a common cause (purpose-bound, redeemed by doing). Others accept promises because they know who promises and see what that person has kept. Whoever vouches for someone accepts their promises. Whoever redeemed, or saw it done, confirms it; every promise kept becomes a piece of trust. Anything larger than two is a circle: a shared intention and the space in which promises towards it are visible. A circle does nothing; people do everything.

## Projects

### Real Life Trust Protocol

Identity from encounter, groups as encrypted shared spaces, and the acts of recognising and trusting between people. In the model it carries the person, the circle, the confirmation and the vouch. Identity, encounter, groups and delivery exist; confirmation, vouch and the promise tasks remain to be cast. Open: under which anchor confirmations travel between groups. Page: [projects/rltp.md](projects/rltp.md)

### Human Money Core

Personal vouchers as software: any backing, expiry date, transferable, offline, no blockchain. In the model it carries the liquid promise. The software exists; pools are on its roadmap. Open: how its voucher and log become the promise credential under RLTP anchors, and whether a promise lives on one device or all. Page: `projects/human-money-core.md`, to be written with the project. Seam: [seams/promise.md](seams/promise.md)

### cosmolocal.credit

Commitment pooling: vouchers as personal promises, pools that clear them, in practice in Kenya since 2010. In the model it carries the balance between circles where chains do not close. Open: whether pools are needed at the edge of a circle at all once vouching exists, and how its pools and Human Money Core's planned pools relate. Page: `projects/cosmolocal.md`, to be written with the project.

### Real Life Stack

The app in which all of this runs in daily life: map, offers and needs, groups as shared spaces, confirming and passing on as easy as paying by card. Running. Comes in with the edges. Seam: `seams/daily-life.md`

### Land, legal form, Steiner

Not part of the model. They come in later as edges: land held in common as the condition under which the model sustains itself, the legal form a community chooses, and the link to associative economics for the conversation with the GLS Bank Foundation.

## Inspirations

Not projects of the consortium. Systems the model takes something from.

### GeDANKE

Self-created paper coupons with expiry date and a personal ledger, by Alexander Mok and Renatus Nölle, public domain. The model takes from it the self-created promise, the expiry date, the giver's ledger, and the distinction between inside and outside. Not continued in this form since Alexander Mok's death on 23 November 2025. Page: [projects/gedanke.md](projects/gedanke.md)

### Circles

Every person mints their own currency, one unit per hour, and trust decides whose currency is accepted; payments travel along trust paths by swapping units. The model takes from it the trust path as reach: a promise travels as far as the one who gives it has disclosed their trust, extended by those who vouch for them. The difference: Circles swaps equal units, in the model the same promise travels and vouching takes it back. Page: [projects/circles.md](projects/circles.md)
